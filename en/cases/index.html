From core development support for the high-speed payment blockchain UPCX to collaborations with companies and research institutes.

UPCX

UPCX Development Support

We support the core development of UPCX, a high-speed blockchain built for payments and financial services. Since forming a business alliance with UPCX-Platforms PTE. LTD. in November 2023, we have provided technology and supported the mainnet launch. Learn more at the official UPCX website.

01

What Is UPCX?

UPCX is a high-speed layer-1 blockchain specializing in financial services and payments. It provides digital asset creation and management, smart contracts, and more — building an environment anyone can use with confidence, convenience, and ease.

02

How UPCX Works

UPCX refines Graphene blockchain technology for finance and payments, combining DPoS and BFT to achieve performance on par with — or better than — major credit card networks.

03

PAYCLE's Role

PAYCLE has supported UPCX's core development from the early days, playing a key role in technology selection and problem-solving, backed by years of research in blockchain and finance.

USAP Project

USAP Project

In October 2024, PAYCLE, UPCX-Platforms PTE. LTD., and the Japan Association for Smart Sensing Technology formed a business alliance to pursue secure, efficient data management and analysis through the fusion of sensor and blockchain technologies.

01

Sensing Train

An IoT-focused sensor system modeled on a train, linking sensor modules as connected "cars." The collected data is secured on a blockchain to ensure its reliability.

02

USAP Platform

Sensor data is encrypted, with data records and user information managed on a blockchain. The long-term goal is a platform where sensor data can be provided and used securely.

03

SENSOR EXPO JAPAN 2025

In September 2025, we exhibited a Sensing Train prototype at Sensor Expo Japan 2025. A special lecture by the UPCX co-founder, "Blockchain × Sensors × IoT," also drew strong interest.

Partnership

Corporate Partnerships

We build technology and business partnerships with companies and organizations, centered on blockchain and crypto assets.

fantasista, Inc.

A partnership with fantasista, a company expanding into new businesses from its real estate foundation. Together we are introducing stablecoin payments for hotel accommodation, issuing security tokens in the real estate and hotel businesses, and jointly exploring RWA (real-world assets) such as precious metals, art, and trading cards, as well as the fan economy.

YAMAKOMA ASSET BANK Inc.

A three-party memorandum on capital and business alliance in the trading card field, signed with fantasista and YAMAKOMA ASSET BANK. By applying UPCX technology to the vintage card market, we aim to create a new trading marketplace connecting investors and collectors worldwide.

Bitcoin Savior Co., Ltd.

A business alliance on the recovery, preservation, and utilization of crypto assets with Bitcoin Savior (Ureru Net Advertising Group), a company specializing in recovering inaccessible crypto assets. The partnership spans support for payments and transfers of recovered assets, Web3 business development, and sharing of project opportunities in Japan and abroad.

Nahdlatul Ulama

A partnership with Nahdlatul Ulama, Indonesia's largest Islamic organization. Ahead of anticipated regulatory easing on stablecoins, we are running a token payment proof of concept at a university affiliated with the organization — building local insight toward real-world implementation in the Indonesian market, one of the world's most populous.

Japan Association for Smart Sensing Technology

A three-party alliance with the association — which promotes sensor R&D and real-world implementation — and UPCX-Platforms. Together we drive the USAP project, managing and analyzing sensor data securely and efficiently on a blockchain, and building an IoT-focused sensor system and data distribution platform.

Nagasaki Institute of Applied Science

Through an industry–academia partnership, we established the "Green Blockchain Development PAYCLE / UPCX Joint Research Program." Together we pursue applied research aimed at real-world implementation, including blockchain solutions for environmental and social challenges such as renewable energy, and the implementation of post-quantum cryptography on blockchains.
