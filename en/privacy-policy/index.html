Privacy Policy

This website introduces the business activities of PAYCLE Inc. (the "Company").

Personal Information Protection Policy

The Company has established the following personal information protection policy. We maintain systems for protecting personal information and ensure that all employees understand its importance and act accordingly, thereby promoting the protection of personal information.

Management of Personal Information

The Company keeps customers' personal information accurate and up to date, and takes the necessary measures — maintaining security systems, developing management frameworks, and providing thorough employee training — to prevent unauthorized access, loss, damage, alteration, or leakage of personal information, managing it strictly and securely.

Purpose of Use of Personal Information

On this website, we may ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, and telephone number when you contact us. Such information will not be used for any purpose other than that for which it was provided. Personal information entrusted to us will be used to contact you, provide information about our services, and respond to your questions, by email or by sending materials.

No Disclosure or Provision to Third Parties

The Company manages the personal information entrusted to us appropriately and will not disclose it to third parties except in the following cases:

  • ・When the customer has given consent
  • ・When disclosure is made to contractors engaged by the Company to provide services requested by the customer
  • ・When disclosure is required by law

Security Measures

The Company takes comprehensive security measures to ensure the accuracy and safety of personal information.

Inquiries by the Individual

If you wish to review, correct, or delete your personal information, we will respond after verifying your identity.

Compliance and Review

The Company complies with applicable Japanese laws, regulations, and other standards concerning the personal information it holds, and reviews and improves this policy as appropriate.
