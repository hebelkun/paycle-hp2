About Us

Enriching everyone's lives through technology.

Philosophy

Corporate Philosophy

PAYCLE believes new technology should not belong only to specialists. We aim to deliver it to society as products and services that anyone can use with confidence — expanding the possibilities of people and businesses, and building a more prosperous society through technology.

01

Technical Excellence

Drawing on years of experience designing and developing systems with cutting-edge technology, we deliver systems that combine security with ease of use.

02

Outstanding Network

We work with universities, research institutes, companies, and experts across fields to take on challenges no single company could solve alone.

03

Innovative Mindset

We value a culture of constantly exploring new possibilities and challenging ourselves to find better ways, unconstrained by how things have been done before.

Message

Message from the CEO

"To be a company loved by our customers, through services built from the user's point of view."

We believe technology only has value when it serves the people who use it. PAYCLE has always gone beyond researching new technologies such as blockchain and AI — we are committed to delivering them as working solutions within our customers' businesses and society at large.

No matter how advanced a technology may be, it must never be confusing or difficult for the people who use it. We always stand in the user's shoes, striving for products and services that are secure, easy to use, and trusted for the long term. Together with companies, universities, and research institutes, we will continue turning new technology into prosperity for society.

Masafumi Murakami, CEO

CEO

Masafumi Murakami

After founding a car dealership in 2010, he expanded into public infrastructure, new energy, food service, and more. Drawn to blockchain's potential in finance and payments, he founded PAYCLE in 2018. He also serves as a Special Professor at Nagasaki Institute of Applied Science.

CTO

Fabian Mezarina

Roughly a decade of platform development across blockchain, AI, FinTech, and SaaS. As founder of Meetlabs, he led teams in Japan and Latin America, driving products that power corporate digital transformation. He joins projects with a focus on creating value.

CFO

Yoshiaki Mishima

Experienced in financial products trading at UBS and securitization at HVB Capital Securities. Led fundraising and IPO preparation at Xartec Technology. He contributes his financial expertise and network to our finances and the growth of the blockchain ecosystem.

Director

Kasuga Eto

After working as a researcher at a Nissan-affiliated parts maker, he developed control systems within the Mitsubishi Heavy Industries group, served as a professor at Nagasaki University and CTO at several companies, and now continues his research as a Specially Appointed Professor at Nagasaki Institute of Applied Science.
